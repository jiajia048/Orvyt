Orvyt Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Orvyt helps you understand your sleep, stress and daily rhythm from Apple Health, keep a short journal, and stay close to a few friends. This policy explains what Orvyt collects, why, where it is kept, and the choices you have. "Orvyt", "we" and "us" mean the developer of the Orvyt app.

The short version

  • Orvyt reads Apple Health data to give you scores and reports. Raw Apple Health data stays on your iPhone.
  • Your journal, meals, workouts, period and reports are stored on your iPhone. If you have an account and backup is on, they are also kept in an end-to-end encrypted backup that only you can open.
  • We never sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • AI features are optional. If you turn them on, your health summary is sent to Google's Gemini service to write your reports and answer your questions. Your questions and chats are kept only on your iPhone.

Information you give us

Account: your email address, your password and the name you choose. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider and is never stored in a form we can read. Each account gets an Orvyt ID that friends use to find you.

Personal information: gender, date of birth, height and weight, each of which you can skip. They are kept on your iPhone and in your encrypted backup and are used to personalize your experience. Choosing Female adds period logging.

Things you log: journal entries, moods, meals, workouts you add yourself and period days. Voice entries are turned into text on your iPhone by Apple's speech recognition; the recording is not saved. Meal photos are used only to recognize food (on your iPhone, or by the AI service if you turned AI on) and are discarded after you save the meal.

Apple Health data

With your permission, Orvyt reads health data such as sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, wrist temperature, steps, activity and workouts. Orvyt only reads; it never writes to Apple Health.

This data is processed on your iPhone to create your scores, insights and reports. Raw Apple Health data is never uploaded to our servers. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties, except in the two cases you choose: what you share with friends in Orbit, and the AI features described below.

You can change or remove Orvyt's access at any time in the Health app (Profile, Apps, Orvyt) or turn off reading in Orvyt under More, Apple Health.

Orbit (friends)

If you create an account you can add close friends. A friend request must be accepted before you become friends. Friends can see your name, your Orvyt ID and only the items you turn on in Orbit settings for that day: your mood, a one-line sleep and stress status, meals, workouts and steps. Mood is on by default; everything else is off until you turn it on. We also keep friend requests, friendships, the emoji reactions you send, and notifications about them. You can remove a friend, or block and report someone, from their card in Orbit; a block is kept on our server so you can't find or add each other, and a report (the person's name and what they shared with you that day) is kept so we can review it.

Encrypted backup

When backup is on, your journal, meals, workouts, period, weekly reports, personal information and settings are encrypted on your iPhone before they are uploaded. The key that unlocks them is protected by your password, so we cannot read your backup. Conversations with the AI and Apple Health data are not backed up. If you forget your password and lose your iPhone, the backup cannot be recovered. You can turn backup off at any time in More.

AI features (optional)

Orvyt can use AI to write your reports, answer questions about a report, chat with you in Ask Orvyt, and recognize food in a meal photo. AI is off until you agree to it, and you can turn it off at any time in More.

When AI is on, Orvyt sends the following through our server to the Google Gemini API: your health summary for the day and the last 7 days (such as sleep duration and stages, sleep and stress scores, heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing rate, blood oxygen, wrist temperature, steps and workouts, and how they compare with your usual); the questions or messages you type, along with the most recent earlier messages in the same conversation so Orvyt can follow it; the mood, meals and workouts you logged that day; your first name and the profile basics you chose to fill in (age, gender, height and weight), so Orvyt can speak to you personally; and a meal photo, only when you use photo recognition. Your email, Orvyt ID, the text of your voice notes and your period are never sent.

Google processes this data under its paid Gemini API terms, does not use it to train its models, and may keep it for a limited time to detect abuse. Our server does not store the content of these requests or your photos; it keeps only a daily count of how many AI requests you made, to apply fair-use limits. When AI is off, reports are written on your iPhone from templates and food in photos is recognized on your iPhone.

Your questions about a report and your chats in Ask Orvyt are saved only on your iPhone so you can look back at them: questions stay with the report they belong to, and Ask Orvyt keeps the last 30 days. They are not stored on our server and are not included in your backup. You can clear your Ask Orvyt history at any time in Ask Orvyt.

Service providers

We use Supabase to run accounts, Orbit and the encrypted backup, and Google (Gemini API) for the optional AI features. They process data only to provide these services to us. Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where these providers operate.

How long we keep data

Morning and evening reports are kept on your iPhone for 7 days. Weekly reports and your journal are kept for one year, on your iPhone and in your backup. Questions about a report are kept with that report. Ask Orvyt chats are kept on your iPhone for 30 days. What you share in Orbit is replaced each day. Notifications are kept for 30 days. Your account information is kept until you delete your account.

Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your personal information in More. You can delete everything Orvyt stores on your iPhone and in your backup with More, Delete all my data. You can delete your account with More, Delete account; this removes your account, friends, shared items, notifications and backup from our servers. You can turn off backup, sharing, AI and Apple Health access at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights, such as asking for a copy of your data; contact us to make a request.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Account data is protected by access rules so each person can only reach their own records, and backups are end-to-end encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Children

Orvyt is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Not medical advice

Orvyt is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If something feels wrong, talk to a doctor.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
